FinCalc Pro — Privacy Policy

Applicable to FinCalc: 1099 Tax & Loan Calc (com.vashudev.fincalc)

Effective Date: November 1, 2026  |  Developer: vashudev (Raghavendra Ojha)

🔒 Zero Data Transmission: FinCalc Pro operates 100% offline. All financial numbers, income amounts, business deductions, filing statuses, and mortgage numbers are processed strictly on your local device. We never transmit, store, or sell your financial data.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how FinCalc: 1099 Tax & Loan Calc ("we", "our", or "the App") handles user information. We are committed to absolute user privacy and transparency.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because the application is designed to be private and offline-first:

  • We do NOT collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • We do NOT transmit your gross income, expenses, tax filings, or loan details to external servers.
  • We do NOT maintain user accounts, user databases, or cloud profiles.

3. Local Data Storage & PDF Reports

All calculations (1099 Self-Employment Tax, Schedule SE estimates, IRS Form 1040-ES quarterly deadlines, and loan amortization schedules) are performed entirely in-memory on your device.

When you choose to export a PDF report, the file is created locally in your device's temporary application cache directory (cacheDir/reports/). This file is shared strictly via standard Android system share intents when initiated by you. You have complete control over where the PDF is sent or saved.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development while keeping the application free, the App integrates Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDKs:

  • Google AdMob: May process standard device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), IP address, and general diagnostic telemetry to serve non-personalized or personalized ads in accordance with your privacy choices.
  • Google UMP: Used to collect and manage privacy consent for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and other jurisdictions subject to GDPR/CCPA.

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

5. Device Permissions

FinCalc Pro requests only the minimal necessary Android permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively by Google Play Services to request advertisements and consent dialogs. The calculation engines function completely in Airplane Mode without internet access.

6. Children's Privacy

FinCalc Pro does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). The application is intended for adult professionals, freelancers, and home buyers.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Because we do not store personal data on any server, there is no personal data for us to access, rectify, or delete. You may reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID).

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
