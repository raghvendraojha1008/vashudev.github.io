Privacy Policy

StreakFlow: Habit & Fasting (Package: com.vashudev.streakflow)
Last Updated & Effective Date: November 1, 2026

100% Privacy & Offline First: StreakFlow does not require user registration, passwords, or personal email addresses. All your habit check-ins, sobriety timers, and fasting logs remain stored exclusively in the private SQLite database of your Android device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how StreakFlow ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Raghavendra Ojha / Vashudev, handles your information when you use our mobile application on Google Play.

Your privacy is our core design priority. Unlike typical habit and fasting trackers that upload personal daily routines to cloud servers, StreakFlow is engineered as an offline-first tool.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Personal Data: We do not collect, request, or store any personal identity data such as your name, email address, physical location, phone number, or contacts.

Habit & Health Tracking Data: Information you enter into the application (habit titles, check-off epoch dates, intermittent fasting start/stop timestamps, and sobriety financial savings amounts) is stored strictly in your device's local encrypted/clean SQLite Room database. This data never leaves your physical device and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Monetization

To support independent development without charging monthly subscriptions, StreakFlow displays non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-sensitive device data, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Coarse geographic location (country or state level based on IP address)
  • App performance, ad impressions, and crash diagnostics

We implement the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to ensure complete compliance with European GDPR, UK GDPR, and California CCPA/CPRA consent regulations. Users are prompted upon initial launch to choose personalized or non-personalized ad preferences.

Review Google's Privacy Policy and Ad Terms here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Android Permissions Used

StreakFlow requests the minimum permissions required for operation:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely by Google AdMob to serve advertisements and verify user consent status.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to deliver optional local notifications when your intermittent fasting window is complete or to remind you of daily habit check-ins. Notifications are generated locally by your device via Android WorkManager.
  • VIBRATE: Used to provide tactile, responsive haptic feedback when you complete a streak milestone or check off a habit.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your tracking data resides solely on your Android device, you have complete ownership and control over it:

  • Instant Complete Deletion: You can permanently delete all habit and fasting logs at any time by clearing the app data via Android Settings > Apps > StreakFlow > Storage > Clear Data, or by simply uninstalling the application.
  • Advertising ID Reset: You can reset or delete your device's Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

6. Children's Privacy

StreakFlow is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this public webpage with an updated effective date.
